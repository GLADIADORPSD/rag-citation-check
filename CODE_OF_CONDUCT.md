# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, contracts, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or ban contributions or participants whose behavior
is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct issues privately through the same channel documented in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), using `[conduct]` in the report title. Do not publish sensitive details
in an issue. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
